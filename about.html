About

The student chapter behind every expedition.

e.quinox is a student-led humanitarian society based at Imperial College London. Every project begins here - designed and built by engineers on the committee, then delivered on expedition in partnership with Rwandan communities.

How we work

Designed at Imperial. Built in Rwanda.

Engineering at Imperial

The committee prototypes each project throughout the academic year at Imperial - solar, electronics, water systems, embedded monitoring. Students get hands-on experience solving problems whose answers will be used.

Field-tested on expedition

Every summer a team takes the design to Rwanda for pilot installation and field feedback. Real sites, real partners, real constraints - the only way to ship engineering that lasts.

Handed over, not owned

Once a project is working, it’s handed over to Rwandan partners who run and maintain it. e.quinox exists to kick-start solutions, not to extract value.

Governance

How we stay accountable.

Imperial College oversight

e.quinox is registered with Imperial College Union and reports annually on finances, safeguarding, and expedition risk assessment.

Rwandan partners

Projects are co-designed with local partners including the Ministry of Infrastructure and the University of Rwanda, ensuring cultural fit and regulatory alignment.

Alumni network

Past committee members form an advisory network that helps current students with technical reviews, industry connections, and continuity between years.
