Partner with e.quinox

A rainwater harvesting solution for sustainable futures in Rwanda.

We are e.quinox, a platform from Imperial College London dedicated to co-creating sustainable, community-driven solutions in rural Rwanda.

In 2010, our first project in Rwanda helped launch what became Bboxx, now delivering clean energy to over 25% of the country. We’re returning. Not with energy, but with water.

The ask

During visits in 2022 and 2024, we met over 100 families and local leaders in Rwanda’s hills. Their message was clear: we need a local, reliable water source.

The first system is in. The next step is a pilot across more households, and that takes partners. Your support directly improves the lives of families working towards a brighter future.
