Partner with e.quinox

A rainwater-harvesting solution for sustainable futures in Rwanda.

We are e.quinox - a platform from Imperial College London dedicated to co-creating sustainable, community-driven solutions in off-grid regions of Rwanda and beyond.

In 2010, an e.quinox project in Rwanda helped launch what became Bboxx, now delivering clean energy to over 25% of the country. We’re returning. Not with energy, but with water.

The ask

During visits in 2022 and 2024, we met with over 100 families and local leaders in off-grid communities in Rwanda’s hills. Their message was clear: “we need a local, reliable water source.”

Help us empower these communities. Your support directly improves the lives of families working towards a brighter future.

What to reach out about

  • Partnership - NGOs, manufacturers, research labs, funders.
  • Imperial student involvement - joining the 2026 chapter.
  • 2026 expedition applications and field-trial volunteers.
  • Donations - individual, institutional, and climate-fund channels.
  • Speaking invitations, press, and media enquiries.

Project timeline

  • 2022 to 2025: surveys in Gakenke, feasibility study, and design of the 700 L modular system.
  • Spring 2026: first system installed in Minazi Sector; business case presented in April.
  • Next: a multi-household pilot, then scale across districts.
