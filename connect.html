Let’s make the next project together.

Whether you’re an Imperial student, an NGO partner, or a donor — the committee reads every message.

What happens next

A committee member replies within seven days, usually from the President or Outreach lead. For partnership and press enquiries we loop in the relevant team member before responding.

What to ask about

  • Partnerships — NGOs, manufacturers, research labs.
  • Imperial student involvement — joining the chapter.
  • 2026 expedition applications.
  • Speaking invitations, press, and media.
  • Donations and support — individuals and institutions.
